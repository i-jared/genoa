Your people. Your conversations. Your time.

Genoa helps friends and family stay close through private video, voice, photo, and text messages. Share a moment now, and let the people you choose catch up when they can.

MAKE ROOM FOR EVERYDAY MOMENTS
Record a video hello, send a voice message, or share a photo. Keep direct conversations and small groups together in one place.

CATCH UP YOUR WAY
Find new messages, browse a conversation's filmstrip, change playback speed, and keep listening in the background. Watched status helps you see what you have caught up on.

KEEP THE CONVERSATION GOING
React to a message, bookmark something to revisit, or set a reminder. A private scratchpad helps you remember what you want to say next.

STAY IN CONTROL
Choose your recipients, manage conversation membership and notifications, and block or report someone when needed. Account export and deletion are available inside the app.

Genoa is currently an invite-only beta. You need a beta invitation to create an account. Camera and microphone access are requested when needed for recording; saving to your photo library is your choice.

Learn more at https://talkgenoa.com.
